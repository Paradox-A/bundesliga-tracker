Defensive Strength: Clean Sheets

A clean sheet is a game where a team doesn't concede at all. It's the single clearest sign of defensive solidity — Bundesliga title winners are almost always among the league's clean-sheet leaders, because you can't lose a game you don't concede in.

Home Fortress vs. Road Warriors

Some teams are much stronger at home than away (or the reverse) — this is one of the oldest storylines in football, and the Bundesliga is famous for it given the size and atmosphere of grounds like Dortmund's Signal Iduna Park. Points per game (PPG) at home vs. away shows exactly how lopsided that split is. Form is the last 5 results (most recent last) — a better read on momentum than the season-long record.
